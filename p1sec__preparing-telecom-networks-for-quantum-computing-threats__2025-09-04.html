For telecom operators, this isn’t a distant theoretical risk—it’s a ticking clock against legacy cryptography. Mobile networks run on a mix of public key infrastructure, symmetric encryption, and signaling protocols that were designed long before anyone thought Shor’s algorithm could break RSA and ECC.
