Press Release P1 Security Reboots Paris, France – January 30th 2024 P1 Security, a pioneer in mobile network security solutions, today unveiled a comprehensive brand remodeling, signaling a new era of growth and innovation for the company. With a renewed mission to safeguard the critical mobile infrastructure of operators and nation-states, P1 Security is poised to become the industry leader in mobile network security. "We are excited to unveil our brand remodeling, which shows our commitment to delivering unparalleled mobile network security solutions now leveraging the latest technologies (AI & ML for anomaly detection and LLM-based expert AI assistants, Kubernetes and other Cloud Native projects, Memory safe languages detection and Binary Analysis for Supply Chain Risk Management) in our solutions" stated Philippe Langlois, CEO of P1 Security. Furthermore, the transition to 5G has brought about a new set of security challenges, particularly around migrating Network Function Virtualization (NFV) to new Cloud-Native containerized 5G infrastructure, both in 5G Cores (SBI based), 4G legacy migrations and OpenRAN experimentations and deployments where operational technologies meet the regular IT stack. P1 Security is at the forefront of addressing these challenges. The company's unique skill-set and experience allows comprehensive identification and detection capabilities for NFV-based networks, including intrusion detection and prevention, vulnerability scanning, and threat intelligence. This new approach will enable our customers to efficiently, verifiably, and accurately achieve compliance with the emerging 5G Security Regulations and Guidance Frameworks established by numerous government regulators and security agencies. P1 Security is also conducting new product pilots with select operator customers to demonstrate the future of 5G security automation.  These pilots utilize a new approach to 5G security that leverages the above mentioned technologies to automate many of the tasks that are currently manual, including vulnerability scanning, threat detection, and policy enforcement.
