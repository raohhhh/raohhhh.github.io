Consolidation is a key driver for operational efficiency. By selecting a AAA with integrated critical adjacent functions—such as a high-performance session database and DHCP server—operators can eliminate the need to license, patch, and maintain separate systems. This ‘all-in-one’ approach not only lowers the hardware footprint but also simplifies the troubleshooting path, as support teams don’t need to correlate logs across different vendors to debug a simple connection issue. The Benefit: This streamlines configuration, troubleshooting, and day-to-day operations, freeing up valuable engineering resources.
