本文深入分析了5G核心网中SMS over NAS（NAS上短信）的工作原理及其面临的安全风险。 - **背景**：在5G独立组网中，短信通过NAS信令在终端和核心网之间传输，由SMSF（短消息服务功能）负责处理。虽然SMS over NAS将短信服务转移到了服务化架构（SBA）上，但并未消除遗留的互连风险，因此5G短信同时继承了SBA的新风险和SS7/Diameter的旧风险。 - **攻击原理与详情**： 1. **移动终结路由风险**：SMS-GMSC需查询服务节点以投递短信，若来自互连的路由查询未被过滤，用户的当前服务节点信息（如SMSF地址）会像5G之前一样发生泄露（身份和位置暴露）。 2. **SMSF注册完整性风险**：移动终结短信依赖UDM中保存的正确SMSF注册信息。这类似于遗留网络中的位置更新过程，在SS7中常被滥用。若SBA认证和授权未严格执行，不受信任的网络功能可能会篡改或替换SMSF注册。 3. **策略不一致风险**：用户可能通过NAS、IMS或遗留路径接收短信。若垃圾短信控制、发件人验证、速率限制和钓鱼短信过滤在不同路径上执行不一致，攻击者会寻找最弱的路径进行滥用。 4. **无线电边缘风险**：SMS over NAS无法阻止伪基站将设备降级到旧无线电技术并推送短信，从而绕过核心网控制。 5. **可用性与计费风险**：SMSF是计费点和可用性依赖点。N20接口过载（如来自受损设备的上行消息洪泛或来自互连的下行流量洪泛）可能导致服务降级；计费不一致可能导致收入流失或成为滥用指标。 - **攻击特征**：攻击并非针对NAS协议本身的全新攻击，而是通过新接口触发的传统SMS信令问题。攻击者利用5G核心网与遗留互连（SS7/Diameter）之间的信任边界缝隙，通过路由查询泄露隐私，或利用SBA授权不严篡改注册信息，以及利用多路径策略不一致绕过安全过滤。

英文原标题：SMS over NAS in 5G: how the SMSF works and where its security risks sit

SMS did not disappear when operators built their 5G cores. In a standalone 5G network, short messages travel between the handset and the core inside NAS signalling, and a dedicated network function, the Short Message Service Function (SMSF), takes over the job the MSC and MME used to do for SMS. The direct answer for security teams is that SMS over NAS moves the SMS service onto the service-based architecture, but it does not remove the legacy interconnect that delivers most mobile-terminated traffic, so 5G SMS inherits both the new risks of the SBA and the old risks of SS7 and Diameter. This article explains how SMS over NAS works, which interfaces the SMSF exposes, where the trust boundaries sit, and what operators should assess and monitor. It is a deep-dive from our wider coverage of SMS-based attacks in mobile networks , focused on the 5G core function that most security programmes have not yet looked at closely. What the SMSF does in a 5G core The SMSF is the 5G core network function responsible for SMS over NAS. 3GPP TS 23.501 lists its responsibilities: checking SMS management subscription data, conducting SMS delivery, running the SM-RP and SM-CP protocols with the UE as defined in TS 24.011, relaying short messages towards the SMS-GMSC, SMS-IWMSC or SMS router, generating SMS charging records, and supporting lawful interception. In practice it is the anchor for every short message that a 5G-registered device sends or receives over NAS. The SMSF does not talk to the radio network. It sits behind the Access and Mobility Management Function, which carries SMS payloads to and from the UE inside NAS transport messages. When a UE registers and indicates that it supports SMS over NAS, the AMF checks the subscription and, if SMS is allowed, selects an SMSF and activates the service for that UE. From that point the AMF behaves as a transport relay and the SMSF owns the SMS logic. Because the AMF sits on the path for every message, the posture described in our AMF hardening guide directly affects the SMS service as well. The SMSF also registers itself in the Unified Data Management function. That registration is what allows mobile-terminated SMS to find the right SMSF later, and it is one of the most security-relevant steps in the whole design, as later sections explain. How SMS over NAS works end to end SMS over NAS is a chain of three segments: NAS between the UE and the AMF, the service-based interface between the AMF and the SMSF, and a legacy or service-based link between the SMSF and the SMS centre infrastructure. Each segment is protected differently, which is why the chain needs to be assessed as a whole. The main procedures, specified in TS 23.502 and in the stage 3 SMSF services of TS 29.540, follow this sequence: During registration the UE includes an indication that it supports SMS over NAS.
