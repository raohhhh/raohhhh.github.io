As technology advanced, the concept of 3G+ networks emerged, often referred to as 3.5G or HSPA (High-Speed Packet Access). With the increase in bandwidth, users could seamlessly stream videos, engage in video calls, and download large files without the frustrating delays that characterized earlier mobile technologies. Moreover, the rollout of 3G+ networks had a profound impact on global connectivity. As more regions gained access to high-speed mobile internet, the digital divide began to narrow, allowing businesses and individuals in developing countries to participate in the global economy. This accessibility fostered innovation and entrepreneurship, leading to the emergence of new services and solutions tailored to local needs.
