Across these interfaces, mobile networks exchange signalling traffic through a collection of interconnect protocols. Historically, much of this signalling relied on SS7 protocols (2G & 3G), 4G introduced the use of the Diameter protocol for LTE roaming and, most recently, HTTP/2-based protocols are used for 5G.
