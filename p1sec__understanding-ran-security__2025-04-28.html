英文原标题：Understanding RAN Security: Comprehensive Analysis and Essential Strategies for Securing Radio Access Networks (RAN)

In today's advanced telecommunications ecosystem, Radio Access Networks (RAN) serve as indispensable gateways, connecting billions of devices globally to core telecom infrastructures. RAN enables seamless mobile communications, supporting fundamental services from basic voice calls and messaging to highly sophisticated applications like 5G, IoT solutions, augmented reality, smart cities, and industrial automation. Given the increasing connectivity demands and digital transformation across multiple sectors, understanding and implementing robust RAN security measures has become more critical than ever before. What is a Radio Access Network (RAN)? A Radio Access Network (RAN) is a fundamental component of mobile telecom infrastructure that facilitates the wireless connection between end-user devices—including smartphones, tablets, laptops, IoT sensors, connected vehicles, and industrial machinery—and the mobile operator’s core network. A typical RAN encompasses essential hardware such as base stations (cell towers), antennas, Remote Radio Units (RRUs), Baseband Units (BBUs), and the underlying software that controls and optimizes network performance. RAN technology has significantly evolved from the early days of GSM (2G) through UMTS (3G) , LTE (4G), and the contemporary deployment of New Radio (NR) technology in 5G networks, dramatically transforming network capabilities and service offerings. Evolution and Different Types of RAN Architectures Understanding the historical context and architectural variations of RAN is essential for grasping current security complexities: Traditional RAN: Historically characterized by hardware-intensive and tightly integrated solutions, traditional RAN infrastructure limits scalability, operational flexibility, and rapid adaptation to changing user demands. Cloud RAN (C-RAN): Represents a significant shift towards virtualization, where baseband processing is centralized into cloud-based infrastructure. This transformation enables greater scalability, cost efficiency, easier management, and quicker service deployment. Open RAN (O-RAN) : A revolutionary shift promoting standardized, interoperable, vendor-agnostic frameworks, allowing telecom operators to integrate hardware and software from diverse suppliers. O-RAN’s open interfaces and modular components promise innovation, reduced vendor lock-in, and enhanced competitiveness but also introduce new security challenges due to increased complexity and broader attack surfaces. Why is RAN Security Crucial? The security of Radio Access Networks is critically important as RAN represents the first line of defense and often the most exposed part of a telecom operator's infrastructure. Compromises at this layer can cascade through the entire telecom network, causing severe service disruptions, loss of user trust, exposure of sensitive personal and commercial data, significant financial losses, and severe reputational damage.
