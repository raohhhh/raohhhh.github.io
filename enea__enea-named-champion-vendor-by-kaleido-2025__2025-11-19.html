Enea Named Champion Vendor for Signaling Security by Kaleido Intelligence For the third consecutive year, Enea has been named a champion vendor by Kaleido Intelligence for its signaling security firewall. The recognition is based on a thorough analysis and assessment of Enea’s firewall, services, and expertise. The Adaptive Signaling Firewall is an intelligence-driven defense used by tier 1 and 2 mobile network operators to protect signaling interfaces on SS7, Diameter, GTP-C, and HTTP/2, as well as voice on SIP/ISUP and CAMEL. It protects networks and user data against signaling attacks and abuse, including protection against location tracking, fraud, interception, and CLI spoofing.
