Modernizing the core A Strategic Guide to AAA Replacement Download Guide For decades, the Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting (AAA) server has been the silent workhorse of the telecom network. As operators move toward increasing data volumes, virtualization, and edge computing, the rigid, hardware-centric AAA appliances of the past are becoming liabilities. This paper outlines the critical considerations for replacing a legacy AAA server, drawing on Enea’s lessons learned from real-world migrations at Tier-1 operators globally.
