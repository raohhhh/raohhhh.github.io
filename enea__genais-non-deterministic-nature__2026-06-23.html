生成式AI的非确定性本质

📋 AI 分析报告

- 攻击事件背景 本文是 Enea 白皮书《Understanding & Managing AI in Network Security》的节选，探讨了生成式 AI (GenAI) 和大语言模型 (LLM) 的非确定性本质如何打破传统确定性 IT 系统的安全原则，从而引入独特的安全风险和漏洞。 - 时间线 文章发布于 2026 年 6 月 23 日，文中引用了 2025 年 OWASP 针对大语言模型和 GenAI 应用的十大风险列表（如 LLM01:2025、LLM02:2025、LLM04:2025），并提及了 2025 年发生的因依赖 AI 幻觉信息而导致知名律师和商业顾问陷入困境的真实案例。 - 攻击原理 传统信息安全基于确定性系统，输入与输出可预测且可控。而 LLM 基于自然语言和预测模型生成内容，具有非确定性。这种非确定性是 AI 超能力的来源，也是其不安全的根源。模型基于自学习的模式生成内容，而非简单检索数据，这导致模型可能产生“幻觉”（以自信的态度输出错误或无意义的信息）。当基于不准确的数据采取行动（尤其是 Agent 行动）时，就会引发安全漏洞。 - 攻击详情 文章详细列举了与 LLM 独特性质相关的几种安全漏洞： 1. 提示词注入与越狱：攻击者在看似无害的查询中插入恶意指令（如暗中要求密送第三方），或使用巧妙的提示诱骗模型忽略预设的安全防护栏。 2. 数据/隐私泄露：攻击者可通过成员推理或大量重复查询提取模型记忆的训练数据，甚至窃取模型本身（研究表明仅需约 10,000 次查询即可窃取模型）。此外，用户误传敏感数据到公共 AI 聊天机器人，或利用受损的 AI Agent 及其工具窃取数据，也会导致数据泄露。 3. 数据投毒：攻击者破坏用于预训练或微调模型的数据，损害模型性能或暴露有害/偏见内容。同时，互联网上充斥的低质量 AI 内容可能导致新模型质量退化（模型崩溃）。 - 攻击特征 利用自然语言作为核心攻击媒介；由于非确定性和幻觉，攻击输出不可预测且难以控制；提示词注入被认定为最紧迫的网络安全挑战，因为它利用了与 AI 应用交互的中心工具，可作为执行其他多种攻击的网关。

AI Security Challenges & Associated Risks – No. 1 GenAI’s Non-Deterministic Nature Resulting AI Vulnerabilities: Prompt Injections and Jailbreaks, Data and Model Poisoning, Data Leakage… Introduction The unique nature of large language models (LLMs) and GenAI applications makes it difficult to apply traditional security principles. It is therefore necessary to understand the nature of AI and the unique vulnerabilities it creates before addressing ways to protect networks from the new risks. The following excerpt is from Enea’s white paper “ Understanding & Managing AI in Network Security “. It looks at GenAI’s non-deterministic nature, the challenges this creates and the associated risks. Magical Feats and Spectacular Fails Conventional information security has been built for deterministic IT systems. Precise, knowable inputs behave in consistent, predictable ways producing replicable – and hence controllable – outcomes (user X can only perform function Y with data source Z…). LLMs exist as sort of counter-culture rebels to this model, which is both their superpower and the ontological root of their insecurity. LLMs’ natural language foundation and non-deterministic framework enable GenAI apps to pull off quasi-magical feats like generating thousands of lines of software code in seconds, but can cause them to fail spectacularly at simple deterministic tasks like counting the occurrences of a particular letter in a word (e.g., ‘how many R’s are in blueberry/strawberry/etc.?’ – fails that made the rounds on social media in 2025). In this anonymous example from Reddit, OpenAI got the number of “r”s correct but failed to convey the right answer visually. This is because LLMs do not simply retrieve data or perform conventional calculations; they use their special token-based NLP framework to generate content based on predictions derived from self-learned patterns. So, while changes in training and model architecture (like forcing a model to always choose the most likely next token, known as ‘setting the temperature to 0’) can reduce hallucinations and make LLMs perform better in deterministic disciplines like math, they can increase the risk of exposing memorized training data, impair their perceived natural demeanor and creativity, and impact LLMs’ superb pattern recognition skills. In any case, one cannot eliminate all the non-determinism from models because they are language-based, and miscommunication is a normal risk of communicating one’s intentions via natural language. For example, in the conversation below, a human (a.k.a. this author) asked Copilot the same math question back-to-back but phrased it differently each time.
